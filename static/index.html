Institutional Masterclass Curriculum (8 Modules)

Module 1: Market Architecture & Mechanics Core Foundations

The Forex market is an over-the-counter (OTC) decentralized network of central banks, liquidity providers, and retail traders.

• Base & Quote: In EUR/USD = 1.0850, EUR is Base, USD is Quote. You pay 1.0850 USD for 1 EUR.

• Bid / Ask / Spread: Bid is your selling price; Ask is your buying price. Spread = Ask - Bid.

• Pip Value Formula: Standard pip is 0.0001 (0.01 for Yen pairs). On EUR/USD, 1 Standard Lot = $10.00 per pip.

Module 2: Precision Lot Sizing & Risk Math Risk Management

Never guess position sizes. Every lot size must be reverse-calculated directly from your technical stop loss distance:

Position Size (Lots) = (Account Balance × Risk %) / (SL Pips × Pip Value)
Worked Example: On a $10,000 account risking 1% ($100) with a 25-pip SL on EUR/USD ($10/pip):
$100 ÷ (25 × $10) = 0.40 Lots.
Module 3: Candle Range Theory (CRT) PipsDoctor Edge

Candle Range Theory treats every closed high-timeframe candle (Daily, 4H, 1H) as a structural range containing 4 reference points:

  • Range High (RH) & Range Low (RL): Prior liquidity extremes.
  • Equilibrium (EQ - 50% Midpoint): The balance point where institutional value resets.

Execution Rule: When price spikes past RH/RL to sweep stops and reclaims the range with a body close inside, enter targeting Equilibrium and the opposing boundary.

Module 4: Advanced Market Structure Smart Money Concepts

• BOS (Break of Structure): Confirmed trend continuation when a candle body closes beyond the previous swing high or low.

• CHoCH (Change of Character): Trend reversal warning. The first candle body close violating the decisive opposing swing level.

• FVG (Fair Value Gap): A 3-candle imbalance where Candle 1 and Candle 3 wicks do not overlap.

Module 5: Global Session Timing & Judas Swings Algorithmic Cycles

• Asian Session (00:00 - 08:00 UTC): Establishes range accumulation and liquidity extremes.

• London Open (07:00 - 10:00 UTC): The "Judas Swing" phase—a fake run that sweeps the Asian high or low before initiating the true daily direction.

• New York Open (12:00 - 16:00 UTC): High-volume expansion session; highest activity on USD pairs and Gold.

Module 6: Gold (XAU/USD) Playbook High Volatility

Gold tracks the price of one troy ounce of gold in USD. 1 point move ($1.00) = 10 pips. A $10 daily expansion = 100 pips.

Inverse correlation: A dropping US Dollar Index (DXY) triggers upside momentum in Gold.

Module 7: Expectancy & Drawdown Recovery Capital Preservation

With a 1:2 Risk/Reward ratio, you only need a 33.4% win rate to break even. A 40–50% win rate produces steady compounding.

Module 8: Professional Trader Psychology Execution Discipline

2-Loss Daily Circuit Breaker: If you take two consecutive losses in a single trading day, shut down the platform immediately to prevent emotional spiral.
